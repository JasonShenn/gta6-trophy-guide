      Only one trophy can be missed

      Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida. The first spot is Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1, and it is gone once Chapter 1 ends. Do it before you leave the chapter, and keep a manual save at the start of each chapter. Everything else on this list can be earned after the story, and every story trophy pops by finishing its mission.

      Save discipline

      • There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so a manual save at the start of each chapter is your only way back.
      • Keep one untouched save from just before the final mission.
      • The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave — rotate the rest freely.
      • Never use cheats on your trophy save: cheats disable trophies and saving.
      • Yago's Chapter 3 race is time-sensitive (hourglass icon). Save first, then race it the moment it appears — it feeds the Strangers & Freaks strand count.
      • 100% completion only asks for 15 of the 18 meditation spots, so the missable spot cannot cost you A Long Road — but it can cost you Positive Mental Attitude.
